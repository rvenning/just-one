# Just One — card dealer

An endless card deck for the party game **Just One**. Deals five numbered words,
you tap the one the guesser called, and it fills the screen while the other four
fade out. That's it — no scoring, no timers. Keep score the way you already do.

**[▶ Open it](https://rvenning.github.io/just-one/)**

Not linked from the family games hub, on purpose — see *After dark* below.

## What it does

- **A card is five random words.** 1,479 of them, dealt from a shuffled bag, so
  no word comes back until every other word has had a turn. You will not see the
  same card twice.
- **Tap the number** the guesser called. That word goes big, the rest drop to
  22% — no more "did she say three or four?" across a noisy table. Keys `1`–`5`
  work too, plus space to deal and `←`/`→` to walk the history.
- **Card history.** Arrows either side of the deal button step back through
  every card dealt this session, in case someone needs to re-check one. Dealing
  a new card from inside the history throws away what came after, same as a real
  deck.
- **Big text** for a wide table, and a **rules summary** for whoever hasn't
  played before.
- **Installs and works offline.** Add it to a home screen and the whole deck
  runs with no signal.

## After dark

A settings toggle swaps the word bank for 640 rude ones — the
[Deep Undercover list](https://github.com/koosvary/codenames/blob/master/word_lists/deep_undercover.txt)
plus about 200 more in the same register. Innuendo-first rather than
shock-first, because a word nobody can hint at isn't a word you can play.

Two deliberate choices:

- It is **off every single time the app opens**. The setting is never saved. A
  phone left on the coffee table can't be picked up already rude.
- Turning it on takes a **confirmation**, not one stray tap.

That's also why this app has no card on `rvenning.github.io` — the girls browse
that page.

## Word banks

| File | Words | Where they came from |
|---|---|---|
| `js/words-sfw.js` | 1,479 | 549 from the fan-made *Just One expansion cards v1.1* print-and-play, plus ~930 written for this app in the same register |
| `js/words-nsfw.js` | 640 | The 390-word Deep Undercover list, plus ~250 written to match |

Both are linted by `tests/words.test.js`: single sentence-cased words, nothing
over 13 characters, no duplicates inside a bank, and a floor on bank size so an
edit can't quietly shrink the deck. Overlap *between* the banks is allowed and
usually the joke (Bacon, Sheep, Candle).

Adding words means editing the arrays and running `npm test`.

## Built on gamekit

Vendored from [rvenning/gamekit](https://github.com/rvenning/gamekit) into
`lib/`. Re-sync with:

```
node "D:\OneDrive\Documents\Claude Code\gamekit\tools\sync-to-game.js" "D:\OneDrive\Documents\Claude Code\just-one"
```

This uses `gk-util`, `gk-ui`, `gk-audio` and `gk-pwa` only. **No `gk-storage`
and no `gk-profiles`** — there are no players, no scores and nothing to sync, so
there is no Firestore collection and nothing in the nightly backup.

One override worth knowing: `gk-base.css` styles `.screen` as a centred,
`position: fixed` column, which is right for a game menu and wrong for a card
that has to fill the phone. `css/style.css` overrides both explicitly.

## PWA files

`manifest.json`, `sw.js` (network-first, cache fallback), `icons/` generated by
`tools/make-icons.js`. **Bump `CACHE` in `sw.js` whenever a shell file changes**
or devices keep serving the copy they already have.

## Tests

```
npm test
```

`node --test`, no framework. `tests/deck.test.js` pins the deck invariants with
a seeded RNG (no repeat until the bag is spent, never a short card or a repeated
word within one card, history back/forward, dealing mid-history truncates,
the bank is never shuffled in place). `tests/words.test.js` lints the data.

## Storage

`localStorage` under `jo:settings` — sound, spotlight and big text only. No
account, no sync, no analytics. After-dark is deliberately not stored.
