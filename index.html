How to play

Just One is co-operative. Everyone wins or nobody does.

  1. One player is the guesser. They must not see the card.
  2. The guesser calls out a number from 1 to 5. Tap it here.
  3. Everyone else secretly writes one single word as a clue.
  4. Compare the clues. Any clue written by two or more people is wiped out — that is the whole game. So is anything that breaks the rules: the mystery word itself, a made-up word, or a different form of the word.
  5. Show the guesser what survives. They get one guess.
  6. Right: keep the card. Wrong: discard it and remove the next card from the deck. Passing costs the card only.

Play 13 cards and score out of 13. This app just deals the cards — keep score however you already do.
